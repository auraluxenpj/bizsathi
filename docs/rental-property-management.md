# Rental Property Management in BizSathi

Rental Property Management is a separate workspace within BizSathi. Open it from **Property Management → Rental Property Management** in the main navigation.

## Setup

1. Sign in with the authorized platform-owner account.
2. Open Rental Property Management and create the independent rental workspace.
3. Add properties and units, then register tenants and create active rental agreements.
4. Enter meter readings and configure shared charge types and allocation rules.
5. Generate monthly invoices. Invoices remain pending until the owner approves them.
6. After approval, share the PDF with the tenant and confirm it was sent. Record full or partial payments against the invoice.

## Access

- **Owner:** full rental workspace control, including invoice approval, sharing, team access and audit history.
- **Accountant:** can maintain rental records, create invoices, record payments and expenses; cannot approve or send invoices.
- **Viewer:** read-only rental access.

Rental records are isolated from Auraluxe retail inventory, sales and accounting records. Existing BizSathi business data and permissions are not changed by this module.
